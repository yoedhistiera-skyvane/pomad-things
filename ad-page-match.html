What "matching" means

Four checks for every ad before it goes to a page

  • Same first lineThe ad's headline or opening words repeat the page's headline. The visitor knows they are in the right place in one second.
  • Same pictureUse the page's hero photo, or a scene from it. Every page below already has one ready.
  • Same actionThe button promises what the page does. "Commander" fits a product page. A quiz or an article needs "En savoir plus".
  • Same numbersPrice per day, number of questions, number of people and the star rating are the same on the ad and on the page.

Meta does not allow ads that say or imply the viewer has a health condition ("vous avez de l'eczéma", "votre eczéma"). The lines below speak in the first person, about the skin in general, or about "mon enfant".

How to test it

One ad set per page, same rules as Test créa

Setup. Each page gets its own ad set in Test créa at €50/day, with the button set to "En savoir plus". The same creative keeps running to the homepage in Ventes_Testing, so we compare the same ad on two pages. Switch off if there is no add-to-cart after €150. A page wins at ROAS (1-day click) of 1.3 or more after €270, and at a lower cost per purchase than the homepage copy.

For Neil

Things on the pages to line up first

  • How many people took the quiz221 732 on the quiz, 202 476 and "200 000" on /raisons/diagnostics. Pick one number and use it everywhere, ads included.
  • How many questionsThe quiz asks 10. /histoire/saskia says "7 questions, un tap par question".
  • Price per day/raisons/taxe says 1,99 € a day. Lettre Saskia, the best ad, says 2,10 € a day.
  • Star rating4,35 / 5 and 4,4 / 5 (3 042 avis) on the same quiz page; 4,4 / 5 on the articles.
  • Which Cure is pickedThe pages push the 3-month Cure (the only one with the 30-day guarantee), but the offer block opens on the 1-month Cure.
  • Quiz page loadingAbout half of the clicks to /diagnostic load the page; across the account it is 57%. Worth checking the load time on a phone.
  • Quiz trackingDoes the quiz send "started" and "finished" to Meta? Without it we can't see where people stop.
  • The 92 % claim"92 % ont réduit ou arrêté la cortisone" comes from a self-assessment of 89 people and sits at the top of 9 pages. Check it before an ad repeats it.
