Pomad Paris · Creative feedback · 6 Nov 2026

Less explaining. More showing.

Pomad sells when it shows the price, Saskia and what real customers say. To scale, we need more of that, made for people we have not reached yet.

What we found

  1. Price, Saskia and customer proof make the money, but get little of it. They return 1.6 to 2.3 on only 13 % of spend (30 days). Give these messages more concepts.
  2. Images beat video at every funnel stage. MOF images 1.36 vs video 0.96. TOF images 1.58 vs video 0.97, yet TOF images get only 2.5 % of spend (14 days). Make more images for new people, not only price images.
  3. AI explainers and long explanations don't sell. The same message did 2.22 with real hands and a customer's message, and 0.64 as an AI cartoon. Ads explaining gut health or cortisone return about 0.77 on a fifth of spend (30 days). AI works in one form so far: a character speaking as the enemy, at 1.54 on €2,654. Show one real thing. If you use AI, use a character.
  4. Short videos with the product early sell. We reviewed 13 working videos and 7 big losers. Under 60 seconds: 8 of 13 vs 1 of 7. Product by second 18: 9 of 13 vs 1 of 7. Brief for this from the start.
  5. Three things do not separate winners from losers. Skin in the first 3 seconds (5 of 13 vs 3 of 7). Saskia on camera (6 of 13 vs 4 of 7). Watch time: losers held more people at 3 seconds (28 % vs 23 %). Judge an idea on sales, and work on the first sentence.
  6. A recut does not bring a winner back. The TikTok reply did 1.98 all-time. Its 56-second recut holds attention best of the 20 videos and did 0.39 over 14 days. Keep the format, write a new script.
  7. We keep reaching the same people. Only 39 % of people reached in October had not seen our ads in September. In August and September it was about 60 %. Keep TOF and MOF at 70 % of spend or more.
  8. There is room nobody uses. Men get 26 % of spend, and people over 55 buy the most per euro. Among 83 competitor ads live in France, none shows a founder who speaks, TV proof, French psoriasis ads beyond the scalp, or a programme with a refund. Use this for the 25 % new.

Iterate: make more of what works

Click any ad to open it. Videos play with sound.

Video · TOF / MOF

1. Make packing-table videos

Do
Film hands at the packing table with no face. Read one customer message, explain In & Out with two Post-its, show the pack, then close the box. Aim for about 50 seconds.
Why
"J'ai une dyshidrose" did 2.04 over 14 days and 2.49 over 7. "Hauts et bas" did 2.03 and 2.40. These are our 2 best videos this week.
Try
  • Other conditions: eyelids, hands that crack, psoriasis on the elbows
  • One message per video, opened from a real envelope
  • End on the box closing with the offer on screen

Video · MOF

2. Start with what a customer said

Do
Make the customer's words the first line. Saskia or a real person says them, with the skin on screen beside them.
Why
"Dernier espoir" did 1.82 over 14 days and 1.98 over 7, even though only 15 % were still watching at 3 seconds. "Sa fille se grattait jusqu'au sang" did 1.84 over 7 days.
Try
  • "Je peux enfin dormir la nuit"
  • "Enfin une crème que je supporte"
  • Clear each quote with the customer first

Static · BOF

3. Write new founder letters

Do
Keep the same look: "Je suis désolée" style headline, typed letter, signed by Saskia. One topic per letter.
Why
Lettre Saskia did 3.04 over 14 days and 2.67 over 7 on €2,005. It is our best image at real spend.
Try
  • Why the Cure lasts 3 months
  • A letter to parents
  • A letter about psoriasis
  • A winter letter

Static · BOF

4. Show the price like a receipt

Do
One price device on one image: the crossed-out price, the new price, the pack. Add the price per day.
Why
Offre flash did 4.08 and −30 % did 4.53 over 14 days.
Try
  • The 3-month saving as a till receipt
  • Price per day compared with a daily coffee
  • Black Friday versions, ready by 16 Nov

Static or video · MOF / BOF

5. Answer one objection at a time

Do
Open on the objection in quotes, admit it, then answer. As a static: three ticks. As a video: Saskia replies to a real comment.
Why
"C'est trop cher…" did 2.52 over 14 days. "Je peux pas me permettre de payer Pomad" did 1.80 all-time. One copy faded fast in testing, so make several.
Try
  • "Ça marche vraiment ?"
  • "Encore une crème ?"
  • "3 mois, c'est long"

Static · TOF / MOF

6. Put real skin and a named customer on the image

Do
Combine a real hand or real skin, one short customer quote with a name, and the pack.
Why
"Riche comme un beurre" did 1.52 on €4,557, our biggest ad. Avant Après did 1.65. "Son eczéma lui vole ses nuits ?" did 1.47 over 7 days.
Try
  • New layouts of "Riche comme un beurre"
  • One zone per image: hands, face, legs
  • Open on the moment for TOF: nights, the school run

Video · TOF

7. Explain with one object

Do
Pick one object that explains the idea and show it in the first second. Don't explain the science.
Why
Lego skin barrier: 2.54 all-time. Fire extinguisher for cortisone: 2.40 over 7 days. "Si tu comprends ces trois mots…" did 1.61 over 14 days.
Try
  • A cracked wall for the skin barrier
  • A plant: water for the skin, fertiliser for the inside
  • Three words on screen, one idea

Video · TOF / MOF

8. Cut long founder videos

Do
Take the videos that hold attention but don't sell. Move the product before second 18, add the price, and end on a call to action.
Why
Médecine fonctionnelle went from 0.71 at 79 seconds to 1.22 at 49 seconds with the price on screen, and 1.94 over 7 days.
Try
  • Next to cut: Eczéma dyshidrose short versions (0.88 and 0.89)
  • Keep the first line, cut the science in the middle

Video · TOF

9. Tell one real moment

Do
One feeling or one moment, said in the first sentence by someone who lives it.
Why
Honte did 2.20 over 14 days. "Ce que ça prend", a mother at her kitchen table, did 1.95 over 30 days. "Rentrée de ma fille" did 1.45, but all 6 sales came on one day, so it is not proven yet.
Try
  • "Je veux pas qu'elle cache ses bras"
  • A night with a child who scratches
  • The top you no longer wear

Video + static · TOF

3. Ads made for men and over-55s

Do
Cast and write for them, instead of hoping Meta finds them.
Why
Men take 26 % of spend and have one video made for them. People aged 55–64 return the most per euro. Women over 45 already see our ads 7 to 8 times a month.
Try

  • Over 55: hands and shins, long first-person static

Static · MOF / BOF

4. The Cure, week by week, with the refund

Do
Show what to expect in month 1, 2 and 3, and the 30-day refund.
Why
Most bad reviews say "no change after a month", and the Cure lasts 3 months. No competitor sells a programme with a refund.
Try

  • Confirm the refund terms first

Video · MOF

Do
Put Saskia on camera saying what the letter says. Use the TV moment after the hook, never first.

Using AI: what to build next

Talking characters did 1.54 on €2,654. Cartoon stories did 0.63 on €6,551. AI b-roll with voice-over did 0.60 on €872. Spend on characters is still small, so this is a promising signal, not proof at scale.

Video · TOF

1. Recut "Je suis l'eczéma"

Do
Cut it to about 50 seconds, show the product by second 18 and end on the offer.
Why
This script did 1.51 on €1,966, but it shows the product at about 36 seconds and has no offer.
Try

  • Cut the middle of the explanation
  • Rewrite any line that promises a result

Video · TOF

One character per problem, speaking as the enemy from the first line. Keep the same pace and end on the pack.

Video · TOF / MOF

4. AI hook, then real proof

Do
Open with 5 seconds of the character, then cut to a real customer message at the packing table and the real pack.
Why
It combines our two best video formats. It's untested.
Try
  • Character says the problem, real hands answer it
  • Keep it under 60 seconds

Test first: one Test créa ad set at €50/day with 3 ads: the recut, "Je suis la démangeaison" and "Je suis le psoriasis". Run AI-looking ads from L'équilibre de la peau.

Don't use AI for

  • AI people shown as real customers. That's a fake testimonial and a legal risk.
  • AI skin or AI before/afters.
  • Lines that promise a result. Rewrite "soigner définitivement ton eczéma" in "Je suis la cortisone".

Not working right now, and how to fix it

  • AI cartoon explainers "Le hack" did 0.64 over 30 days.Fix: Keep the message and film it with real hands at the packing table. The same message did 2.04 that way. If you want AI, use a talking character instead.
  • Long interview stories The stress video did 0.83. It runs 139 seconds, names Pomad at about 89 seconds and has no call to action.Fix: Cut it to under 60 seconds, show Pomad by second 18 and end on a clear call to action.
  • Recuts of old winners The recut of the TikTok reply did 0.39.Fix: Keep the comment-reply format and answer a new question with a new script.
  • Long science and cortisone lectures Médecine fonctionnelle at 79 seconds did 0.71. "3 raisons cortisone" did 0.72 over 30 days.Fix: Use cortisone as one line before the product, or explain it with one object. Médecine fonctionnelle reached 1.22 at 49 seconds with the price.
  • Tried-everything stories They did 0.51 over 30 days.Fix: Add proof on screen: real skin or a customer's result. "Dernier espoir" has the same feeling with skin on screen and did 1.82.
  • Ads that only get clicks The before/after GIF had the best click rate in testing and did 0.17.Fix: Use the before/after as proof beside a named customer review and the pack, like Avant Après (1.65).

For every ad

  • Open with a real situation or a customer's words.
  • One message per ad.
  • Real people, real hands, real customer words. Use AI only as a character.
  • Show the product by second 18. Keep videos under 60 seconds.
  • End every video with a clear call to action.
  • Say soothe, comfort, routine. Never treat, heal, cure.
  • Talk about the skin and the moment, not "ton eczéma". Meta can reject it.
  • Name the file with the final ad name.
