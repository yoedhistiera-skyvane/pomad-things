Cost per purchase uses 7-day click.

What the numbers show

  • Video gets more clicks (1.21% vs 0.91%) and cheaper clicks (€0.57 vs €0.65).
  • But fewer of those people buy: 0.66 purchases per 100 clicks for video, 1.19 for images.
  • Half of video purchases are view-through (50%, vs 37% for images): people saw it, didn't click, bought later.
  • So video works as the first touch and images close the sale.

Last 30 days.

The message matters as much as the format

  • Images with a price offer or the founder's letter: 1.55.
  • Images with other messages (education, product): 0.90, close to video.
  • The same price offer in a video: 0.50.

ROAS 1-day click, last 30 days.

What to change

  • Half of new concepts are images. Today it's about a quarter.
  • Images get their own budget in Test créa, so Meta can't move it all to video.
  • Price, founder letter and press go in images. Video is for showing the product, texture, people and results.
  • When a winning image stops getting budget, make the same message in a new scene. A small edit of the same image looks the same to Meta.

Test briefs for next week

Each test keeps a message that already sold and puts it in a new layout or scene. Meta groups ads that look alike and gives the budget to one of them, so a near-copy of a winner gets little spend. A new look gets its own chance, and the message stays the one we know sells.

How every test is judged. Each concept runs in Test créa at €50/day. Switch off if there is no add-to-cart after €150. Winner: ROAS (1-day click) of 1.3 or more after €270. Winners move to Ventes_Testing.
Test 1

Founder letter in three new scenes

What to make
Make 3 images. Keep what Lettre Saskia says: why the Cure takes 3 months, the 3-month price line, Saskia's signature. Put it in a scene the account has not used yet, and write each one to a different reader.
Versions
  • Handwritten letter on a kitchen table, to parents of a child with eczema
  • Email from Saskia in an inbox screenshot, to people with psoriasis
  • Card tucked inside an open Pomad box, to adults whose hands crack in winter
Why
Lettre Saskia Img2 is the best image in the account: €15.0k spent, ROAS 1.99 (1-day click), 369 purchases. It is still live but got only €749 in the last 7 days. Parents and psoriasis get under 1% of spend today.

How this was read

Meta counts only clicks here (1-day and 7-day click). Meta can over-count, so a winner is confirmed in Shopify through utm_content before more budget goes on it. "Last change" is the last edit Meta shows on the ad, which for ads switched off one by one is the day they were paused. Sources and messages are read from ad names. Ventes_Retention is left out because it also reaches past buyers.
