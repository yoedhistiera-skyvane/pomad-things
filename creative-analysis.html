1

Images lead on both windows. Statique_Lettre Saskia (2.01 · 1.43), Le meilleur produit pour (1.83 · 1.52) and Statique Offre (1.34 · 0.95) sell to clickers the same day and over the week.

2

Founder story, offer and social proof are the strongest angles. Founder story 1.85 · 1.29, offer 1.45 · 1.02, social proof 1.40 · 1.16. Root cause (gut-skin, stress) 0.70 · 0.60 and cost / "tried everything" 0.41 trail. The founder letter is itself about price: Saskia explains the 3-month Cure at −30%, €2.10 a day. So the three best images all sell the price.

3

The same angle can win or lose on execution. Cortisone overall is 0.92, but "Timeline dépendance cortisone" reaches 1.33 · 1.17 while "3RY Cortisone", the larger one, sits at 0.80 · 0.60.

4

Small audiences look strong. Psoriasis ads 1.73 · 1.32 and Cure enfant ads 1.68 · 0.99, on less than €1k each. One Adtitude psoriasis image reached 2.65 · 2.01 on €372.

5

The top of the ranking is the same on 1-day and 7-day click. The ads that fall most from 7-day to 1-day are creator videos (marine_boostacom 1.13 → 0.78, Justine 1.01 → 0.43). Their buyers take longer to decide.

6

No sharp fatigue yet, but the best images are seen more and more often. Three images pass 3 views per person in 30 days, and Le meilleur produit pour shows the first drop (−35% in the last 7 days).

7

Video gets most of the budget and most new launches, but images win in 5 of the 6 angles where both run. Videos were 80% of new concepts and 61% of spend, at a ROAS 7-day click of 0.92 vs 1.31 for images. Since 2025, images have cost less per purchase than videos in every half-year.

8

Most new concepts never get enough budget to be judged. Of 103 concepts launched in the 30 days, 23 reached €270 and 3 reached €1,215. The money was there (€40.1k), but two ads took 39% of it.

9

The €29 agency rule no longer fits today's costs. 1 of 191 concepts launched since July meets it, and no live ad does, Lettre Saskia included (€30.6). Comparing sources on ROAS 7-day click in the same period is fairer.

What it means

Video isn't failing as a format. Subtitled education, a creator testimonial and C## UGC clear 1.30 (1.57–1.69). Hook rate and hold rate (3-second views, ThruPlay) weren't in this data, so this can't yet show whether videos lose people in the first seconds or later. A video-metrics pull will add it.

What it means

The account runs on ads created since July, and almost half of the spend is on August ads. The three images that carry results today were created in late July and early August (76–88 days old).
